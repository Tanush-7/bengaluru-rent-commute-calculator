How this is calculated

Rents: guide vs estimate

Guide rate = areas with dense Nov 2026 listings — median asking rent for a semi-furnished flat, usable as a negotiation anchor (±10%). Estimate = fewer listings, wider spread — treat as ±20% and replace with your listing. Gated premium societies run 15–30% higher.

Metro & Bus basis

Metro fare uses BMRCL distance slabs on the station-to-station rail gap (₹10 up to 2 km, rising to ₹90 max), plus last-mile if ticked. BMTC: either a flat monthly pass (amount you set) or per-trip fare × office days — the basis in use is always printed in the verdict, never an unexplained number.

Time & other modes

Peak speeds: Bike ~22 km/h, Auto ~18, Car/Cab ~16, Bus ~14, Metro ~32 plus access. Auto ₹30+₹15/km, Cab ₹70+₹18/km, Own car ~₹12/km, Own bike ~₹5/km, Shuttle ₹2,500/mo pass estimate. All editable in code-level assumptions — treat outputs as estimates, not quotes.
